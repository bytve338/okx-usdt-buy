## What is USDT, and why do people buy it?

USDT, also called Tether, is a stablecoin designed to track the value of the US dollar. It is widely used inside crypto markets because it can act as a quote currency for trading pairs and can be transferred between supported wallets and exchanges.

That does not mean USDT is the same as cash in a bank account. Its value can differ slightly from one US dollar, and the practical risks depend on the issuer, platform, blockchain network, custody arrangement, and local regulations.

Common reasons people buy USDT include:

- Trading other cryptocurrencies against USDT.
- Moving dollar-denominated value between supported platforms.
- Holding a stablecoin instead of remaining exposed to more volatile crypto assets.
- Sending funds to another wallet or exchange.
- Using USDT in decentralized finance or other blockchain applications.

USDT exists on multiple networks. Ethereum, Tron, Solana, Arbitrum, Polygon, and other networks may appear depending on the product and region. The token name alone is not enough when making a transfer. The sending and receiving platforms must support the same network, or the funds may become difficult or impossible to recover.
